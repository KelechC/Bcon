What is BCON? +

BCON is BEYOND Conference, an experience created to help people challenge limiting beliefs, discover purpose, grow in faith, and take meaningful steps towards their future. It's part of the Beyond movement's vision to help people go beyond where they are.

When will BCON take place? +

BCON is scheduled for Tuesday, 22 December 2026. The conference will take place online. The official start time and access instructions will be communicated once confirmed.

Is registration free? +

Yes. Registration for BCON is free. Complete the registration form on this website to register. Your registration is confirmed once you receive an official confirmation.

How do I join the online conference? +

Official participation instructions and the online access link will be shared with registered attendees when they are available. Make sure you provide a valid email address when registering.

Who can attend BCON? +

BCON welcomes people who want to learn, grow, strengthen their faith, discover purpose, and take positive steps towards their future. If you're ready to challenge your limitations and grow, you're welcome to participate.

Will speakers and the programme be announced? +

Details about speakers, session times, and the final programme will be published as they are confirmed. Check this website for official updates.
